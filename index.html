souzinhag publisher

A personal publishing tool for the GTA content creator souzinhag (Gabriel Souza, Brazil).

What it does

souzinhag publisher is a private tool used only by its owner. It takes short videos about the game GTA 6 that the creator has produced and approved, and uploads them to the creator's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, so he does not have to upload each video by hand.

  • Only the owner's own TikTok account is connected. There are no other users.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved by the creator before it is sent.
  • The tool uploads the video file and its caption. It does not read messages, followers or any other data.
