Privacy Policy

souzinhag publisher · Last updated: November 16, 2026

souzinhag publisher ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Gabriel Souza ("souzinhag") to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Data we access

  • TikTok account information: the basic profile of the connected account (open ID, display name and avatar), only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: the OAuth tokens TikTok issues after the owner logs in. They are stored only on the owner's own computer and used only to upload videos.
  • Content: the video files and captions the owner chooses to publish.

3. How we use it

Solely to upload the owner's approved videos and captions to his TikTok account and to check the upload status. We do not sell, share, rent or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and files stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on external servers controlled by the app. Tokens can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

5. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the app is no longer used. To request deletion of any data, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/gabrielsouzaf77-rgb/souzinhag-site/issues.
